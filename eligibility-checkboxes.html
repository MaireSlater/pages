Who can apply

This is a Subway that consolidates all 5 rebates together because, from a user perspective, they behave almost like one rebate:

  • You must apply once for all 5 (or however many you are eligible for) at the same time
  • All 5 share the same form(s)
  • They share most of the same eligibility

What's difficult about the eligibility is that there are 3 main rebates with their own eligibility criteria. The first one is for everyone, and the rest of the rebates have to meet *most* of its criteria.

The second and third rebates must meet the criteria for the first rebate, and also their own specific criteria.

Users may:

  • Only need the first one
  • Need the first and second
  • Need the first and third
  • Need all three

GST/HST new housing rebate

This is the first one

  • What is a substantial renovation?

    This eligibility condition means that you, or someone you hired, substantially renovated or built/constructed a major addition to your existing house, or converted a non-residential property into your house.

    Substantial renovation

    Major changes have to be made to meet the definition of a substantial renovation. In a major renovation project, the interior of a building is essentially gutted. Generally, 90% or more of the interior of the existing housing has to be removed or replaced to qualify as a substantial renovation (the 90% test).

    You do not have to remove or replace the foundation, exterior and interior supporting walls, roof, floors, and staircases to meet the 90% test.

    Only livable areas count towards a substantial renovation, including finished basements and finished attics. Livable areas do not include garages or crawl spaces. Work done to partially complete a basement but not make it a livable basement does not count toward the 90% test.

    Major addition

    This means work on a house that is so significant that the result is viewed as a newly built house. The addition, by itself, would not be considered to result in a newly built house. Significant physical and functional changes also have to be made to the existing house so that the resulting structure would be seen as a newly constructed house.

    The addition should at least double the size of the livable areas of your existing house, which is absorbed into the new one. An example would be making significant structural changes to the appearance and layout of an existing bungalow and adding a full second story.

    Converting a non-residential property

    If you converted a non-residential property into a house the property would generally meet the definition of a substantial renovation for the purposes of this rebate.

  • First resident

    You, or your relation, must be the first individual to occupy the house:

    • If you bought a home from a builder or a share in a co-op: Option 1

      After the construction or substantial renovation of the house is substantially completed

    • If you built your own home: Option 2

      Since the construction or substantial renovation began

      Exception: You made an exempt sale of the house and transferred ownership before it was occupied by anyone.

      For details: Selling the house before anyone occupied it

  • Purchase price or FMV maximums for the GST/HST new housing rebate

    The FMV or purchase price is less than:

    • If you bought a home from a builder or a share in a co-op: Option 1

      If you bought the land
      $450,000 before tax, including the purchase price for a taxable assignment to you of the original purchase and sale agreement (if applicable)
      If you lease the land
      $472,500 when possession was transferred to you, if the builder had to account for the GST at 5% on the self supply of the house

    • If you built your own home: Option 2

      $450,000 at the time that the work is substantially completed, including the land, building and any other structures

    If you live in Ontario

    For houses located in Ontario, you may be eligible to claim the Ontario new housing rebate if you are not eligible to claim the new housing rebate for some of the federal part of the HST only because the fair market value of the housing exceeds $450,000. An Ontario new housing rebate will be available (up to the applicable maximum of $24,000), regardless of the fair market value, as long as all of the other conditions for claiming the rebate are met.

FTHBs' GST/HST rebate

If you meet the eligibility criteria for the GST/HST new housing rebate, except for the FMV amount, you may be eligible for the FTHBs' rebate if you also meet the following:

  • You are a first-time home buyer
    Eligibility criteria for a first-time home buyer

    Generally, to be considered a first-time home buyer, all of the following must be true:

    • You are at least 18 years of age
    • You are a Canadian citizen or permanent resident of Canada
    • You have not lived in a home that you or your spouse or common-law partner owned, or jointly owned, whether in or outside of Canada, as your primary place of residence at any time in the calendar year or in the previous 4 calendar years
      Examples - Calendar year

      If you take ownership of a new home from a builder in 2025, you must not have lived in a home as your primary place of residence that was owned by you or your spouse or common-law partner in the 2025 calendar year, or in 2024, 2023, 2022, or 2021 (the previous 4 calendar years).

      Example 1

      An individual previously lived in a home that they owned. They sold it in June 2022. Since then, the individual has lived in a rental apartment.

      The individual entered into an agreement to purchase a new home from a builder on or after March 20, 2025, and they took ownership of the home in August 2026.

      The individual is not considered a first-time home buyer, as they lived in a home that they owned in 2022, which is in the previous 4 calendar years from the year when they took ownership of the new home.

      Example 2

      An individual previously lived in a home that they owned. They sold it in June 2022. Since then, the individual has lived in a rental apartment.

      The individual entered into an agreement to purchase a new home from a builder on or after March 20, 2025, and they took ownership of the home in February 2027.

      If all other conditions for the rebate are met, the individual would be considered a first-time home buyer, as they did not live in a home that they or their spouse or common-law partner owned in the calendar year or previous 4 calendar years.

    • You meet all of the criteria above on a specific date:
      If you bought a home from a builder or a share in a co-op
      When ownership or possession is transferred to you
      If you built your own home

      • When you first occupied the home as a place of residence

      • When the construction or substantial renovation of the home is substantially completed

  • Neither you, nor your spouse or common-law partner, have previously received an FTHB GST/HST rebate
  • You meet the applicable key dates and FMV maximums
    Key dates and purchase price or FMV maximums for the FTHBs' rebate

    You must meet all of the following conditions:

    • Home purchased from a builder: Option 1

      • You bought the home between March 20, 2025, and 2031
      • Construction begins before 2031
      • Construction finishes and ownership or possession is transferred to you before 2036
      • The FMV is less than:
        If you bought the land
        $1.5 million
        If you lease the land
        $1,575,000

    • Owner-built home: Option 2

      • Construction begins between March 20, 2025, and 2031
      • Construction finishes and you first occupy the home before 2036
      • The FMV is less than $1.5 million
    If you live in Ontario

    Ontario enhanced new housing rebate

    If you meet the eligibility criteria for the GST/HST new housing rebate, except for the FMV amount, you may be eligible for the Ontario ENHR if you also meet the applicable key dates and FMV maximums.

    Key dates and FMV maximums for the Ontario ENHR

    You must meet all of the following conditions:

    • Home purchased from a builder: Option 1

      • You bought the home between April 1, 2026, and March 31, 2027
      • Construction begins before December 31, 2028
      • Construction finishes before December 31, 2031
      • Ownership or possession is transferred to you by December 31, 2032
      • The FMV is less than:
        If you bought the land
        $1,850,000
        If you lease the land
        $2,090,500

    • Owner-built home: Option 2

      • Construction begins between April 1, 2026, and March 31, 2027
      • Construction finishes by December 31, 2029
      • The FMV is less than $1,850,000
